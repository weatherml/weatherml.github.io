We compare two hybrid versions of SPEAR to understand the importance of exposing ML models to coupled ice-atmosphere-ocean feedbacks before implementation into fully-coupled simulations: Hybrid_CPL (with feedbacks) and Hybrid_IO (without feedbacks).
