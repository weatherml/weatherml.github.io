  • Climate downscaling with likelihood functional flow matching¶

    Zhi-Song Liu, Helmi Toropainen, Ammar Kheder, Michael Boy · Oct 2026

    Decisions about heat, frost and flooding are made locally, yet reanalyses and climate models resolve tens of kilometres. Generative downscalers add kilometre-scale detail with uncertainty, but are...Decisions about heat, frost and flooding are made locally, yet reanalyses and climate models resolve tens of kilometres. Generative downscalers add kilometre-scale detail with uncertainty, but are tied to a grid, costly to sample and lack a tractable likelihood. Here we introduce CD-FFM, a functional flow-matching model that treats the high-resolution field as a continuous random field that one model can query at any location and resolution. Its velocity acts pointwise, so spatial variability comes from a noise prior fitted to the observed spectrum and every observation has an exact likelihood. Downscaling ERA5 to 1 km daily temperature over Fennoscandia, CD-FFM lowers the continuous ranked probability score by 27-31% against regression and 12-18% against a forty-times larger neural operator in each of three test years, and outperforms a CorrDiff-style diffusion model while sampling about fifty times faster. The same model transfers to precipitation and makes large kilometre-scale ensembles affordable. more

  • Observation Assimilation Transformer: Probabilistic Weather Analysis from Sparse Station Observations¶

    Christian Donner, Michele Cattaneo, Maxim Samarin, Francesco Zanetta, Icíar Lloréns Jover et al. · Oct 2026

OAT is an encoder-decoder transformer that considers a set of observed locations and their NWP prior to condition predictions at a set of arbitrary unobserved query locations.   • TerraNostra-Bench: Benchmarking Spatial Downscaling and Temporal Rollout for Global 1-km Land-Use Reconstruction¶

    Amirpasha Mozaffari, Marina Castaño, Stefano Materia, Joan Vedrí, Jordi Varela-Agrelo et al. · Oct 2026

    Uncertainty in the terrestrial carbon cycle remains a major constraint in climate projections, partly due to imperfect land surface representations in Earth system models. We propose a data-driven framework for high-resolution land-use reconstruction, treated as a semantic-segmentation task, that combines coarse-resolution scenario data and climate classification with static geophysical features to produce annual land-use maps at 1 km resolution. We introduce TerraNostra-Bench, a change-aware evaluation protocol that separates spatial downscaling from autoregressive temporal rollout and scores models on the rare land-use-change pixels rather than the dominant static background.   • Generative modelling for multivariate spatio-temporal downscaling of monthly climate fields¶

While many methods for spatial downscaling have been proposed, joint spatio-temporal downscaling has received comparable little attention.
