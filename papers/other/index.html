Interferometric and diffractive methods need an undistorted beam and a careful alignment, while most learned approaches treat integer or coarsely spaced charges and do not include turbulence. A Swin-transformer autoencoder compresses thirteen monthly-averaged ocean and atmosphere fields into 32 numbers, trained to reconstruct the fields, with no forecast target.
